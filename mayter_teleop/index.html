Socket tool

Click once to spin at the controller maximum (2000). Click Stop socket to stop spinning while keeping arm tracking active. Switching away from this page also stops the socket.

Motor connections

The socket is expected at ID 8. The tire motor is disabled and is not required.

This check reads motor responses without changing torque, calibration, or IDs. A replacement socket must be assigned ID 8 with that motor connected by itself.


Gripper jaw calibration
Begin to release only the jaw. Place it gently at the usable closed position, mark Closed, then move it to the usable open position and mark Open. Save to update the follower jaw only.
