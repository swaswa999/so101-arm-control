Arm tracking · Two tool motors · Jaw calibration

Connecting…

Match the leader and follower poses before Start. Direct tracking can move immediately. Support the arm before Stop; it releases holding torque.

Tool testing

Test the tools without starting arm tracking. Left/Right control the tire changer; Up/Down control the socket. Both run at maximum configured speed while held. Confirm which tire direction opens and closes your mechanism. To switch sessions, click Stop and release motors first.

Motor connections

Tire tool: ID 7. Socket tool: ID 8. Both are checked before enabling tools.

This check reads motor responses without changing torque, calibration, or IDs. A replacement socket must be assigned ID 8 with that motor connected by itself.


Gripper jaw calibration
Begin calibration, then hold Forward or Backward or move the released jaw by hand. Mark Closed and Open at the usable endpoints, then Save. Forward and Backward indicate motor direction, not open and closed. Stop before the jaw binds.
Maximum configured speed: 2000. Reduced torque remains enabled. Let go to stop and release torque. Losing contact with the page also releases torque. Temporary travel limits are restored on exit unless you save new endpoints.
